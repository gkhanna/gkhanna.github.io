A clearer path.

Choosing a major is a big decision. Start with the essentials, then talk with us about your goals.

Ask a program question ↗
When does the program begin?+

URI plans a soft launch in spring 2027, with the program fully offered in fall 2027. Current URI students can contact the program team about courses that count toward the major.

What makes this an applied AI degree?+

The focus is on using, adapting, and integrating AI tools to solve problems. You’ll combine programming and data skills with domain knowledge, ethical reasoning, and clear communication.

Can I combine IAI with another major?+

Yes. URI describes IAI as a stand-alone degree or a second major alongside another area of study. Work with an advisor to understand course overlap, requirements, and the time needed for your combination.

Will I get practical experience?+

The curriculum includes project-based learning, innovation and entrepreneurship experiences, and a required applied internship. These opportunities help you practice connecting AI skills with real problems and communicating your work.

Do I need to arrive as an AI expert?+

The curriculum includes foundational coursework in AI, programming, mathematics, and statistics. Bring curiosity and a willingness to learn; an advisor can help you understand preparation and course prerequisites. Current URI students need a minimum 2.5 GPA to move into the major.

How many credits will I need?+

The bachelor’s degree requires at least 120 credits. Your individual plan will combine major requirements, general education, and electives. Confirm the final course sequence and any second-major requirements with an advisor.

What should families know about costs and careers?+

Review URI’s current tuition, financial aid, and scholarship information when planning. IAI develops skills relevant to a range of careers; specific opportunities depend on your interests, experience, and the job market.
